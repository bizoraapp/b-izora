# Bizora

**Smart Cash & Credit Sales Management — Offline First**

Bizora is a Progressive Web App (PWA) built for small businesses to manage sales, customers, inventory, and credit collections — fully functional even without an internet connection.

## Features

- 📊 **Dashboard** — real-time overview of cash & credit sales, collections, and daily activity
- 👥 **Customers** — manage customer records and credit balances
- 📦 **Products & Inventory** — track stock levels and catalog items
- 💵 **Cash Sales** — quick point-of-sale cash transactions
- 🧾 **Credit Sales** — record sales on credit and track repayments
- 💰 **Expense Logging** — log business expenses
- 🔁 **Repayment Tracking** — record and monitor customer repayments
- 🖨️ **Reports & Invoices** — print reports and search invoices
- 📴 **Fully Offline** — works without an internet connection, syncs when back online
- 📱 **Installable PWA** — install on desktop or mobile like a native app

## Tech

- Offline-first Progressive Web App (Service Worker + Web App Manifest)
- Deployed via [Netlify](https://bizora-cm.netlify.app)
